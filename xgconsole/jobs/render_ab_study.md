- 2026-08-25 18:44 [renderab-scout] Refreshed the page now that the repair to the existing pipeline has landed, and the result is a stronger finding than before. The repair does fix the laptop that started this, but it reaches only two of seven cases where a shape that should glow renders as an empty room; five remain across four shapes, for at least two different reasons, and the new pipeline renders all five. The page now shows the laptop before and after the repair side by side, plus a figure of every unrecovered case through both pipelines. - 2026-08-25 18:51 [renderab-scout] Found the cause of the second class of black pictures, and it is two separate problems rather than one. Some shapes have a glowing region that covers only part of a surface, and the code refuses to light a surface unless more than half of it glows, so it throws the answer away. Others, like the clock, have surfaces built from just two enormous triangles, and the code samples each triangle at a single point, which misses the digits entirely. The lead we were given, that no faces were being matched, is ruled out by the logs. The repair is smaller than the diagnosis: the work being discarded has already been done earlier in the same function.
- 2026-08-25 19:03 [renderab-scout] Fixed two of the three problems and made the third impossible to miss. The ring and the truck now light up correctly where they were blank before. The clock is still blank, for a third and separate reason: that asset stores several sets of texture coordinates and the code only ever looks at one of them, so the display's surface never reaches the texture it is supposed to paint into. Fifteen of the fifty five shapes store several sets, so this will recur; it now prints a loud named warning instead of passing as an empty picture. Measured the effect of the repair before re-rendering anything: twenty five surfaces across fourteen shapes start lighting up and none stop, so nothing that already worked can break.
- 2026-08-25 20:53 [renderab-scout] Measured what the second repair costs in time, because it is not free: on a chandelier with nine separate surfaces, none of which carry a texture, a single picture went from about two and a half minutes to eighteen. The repair makes every untextured surface do real work where before it did almost none. Added a cheap pre-check that skips surfaces nowhere near any light, verified it leaves the results identical on three shapes, but the chandelier genuinely needs the work so it stays slow.

- 2026-08-26 00:11 [renderab-scout] My explanation for the last two black pictures was wrong and has been withdrawn. I had claimed the asset stored several sets of texture coordinates and the code read the wrong one; checking every material across all fifty five shapes shows all four hundred and thirty seven texture references use the first set, so that theory is dead. The real cause, found by a colleague, is float noise: one corner of the clock's display sits at a hair below zero, which rounds the wrong way and shifts the whole triangle. Verified their repair myself, the display now carries forty one thousand lit pixels where it had none. The number was in my own printout and I read past it. Both pages are being corrected.
- 2026-08-26 01:16 [renderab-scout] Held the page rather than publish a tidy result. Re-testing everything against the newest repair showed that one of the pictures I had reported as recovered was never really lit: it had eighty three stray bright pixels out of a million, and my test for lit only looked at the single brightest pixel. Corrected the test to require real lit area. The honest account is that two of the seven were never pipeline faults at all, their surfaces are pure black so nothing can light them, five were genuine and all five are now fixed. The endpoint is the same, the path to it is not.
- 2026-08-26 01:24 [renderab-scout] Page corrected and republished. A late check turned one of its caveats into an argument: the two phone screens the old pipeline can never light, because the asset paints them pure black, ARE lit by the new pipeline, which takes its colour from the shape's own reconstruction instead of the asset's paint. So that was never a shared limitation of both methods as the page previously claimed, it is a limitation of one of them. Final tally: of seven pictures once counted as faults, two were never faults, five were, and all five are fixed.
